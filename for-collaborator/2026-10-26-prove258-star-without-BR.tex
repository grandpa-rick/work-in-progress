\section*{Claim}
On $LG(m,2m)$ put $s_x(\kappa)=(-1)^{|\kappa|}H_\kappa(-x)$ for $x\in\mathbb{C}$. The negative-degree recursion $(\star)$ (Prop 4.12, \texttt{prop:star})
\[
 s_{x+1}(\kappa)=s_x(\kappa)+\sum_{\rho\subsetneq\kappa}V(\theta_\rho)\,s_x(\rho)\qquad(x\in\mathbb{C}),
\]
where $\theta_\rho=D(\kappa)\setminus D(\rho)$ and $V(\theta)$ is the number of strictly order-preserving maps $\theta\to\{e<o\}$ sending diagonal boxes to $e$, follows from the LS proof of Thm 1.1 alone. No BR, no (G0), no Prop 4.6 enters.

\section*{Proof}
Inputs: (1) $H_\kappa$ is a polynomial (Snapper/Kleiman; also \texttt{lem:P} given (G1)), $H_\varnothing\equiv1$.
(2) Thm 1.1(b), proved in \S3 from (G1)+(LS)+Ehrhart--Macdonald reciprocity: $s_j(\kappa)=N_\kappa(j)$ for \emph{every} integer $j\ge1$ and every strict $\kappa$ with parts $\le m$ (no $\kappa$-dependent lower bound; the zero window is included).
(3) Lemma 4.13 (\texttt{lem:peel}, peeling the top two values, a pure count): for $j\ge1$, $N_\kappa(j+1)=N_\kappa(j)+\sum_{\rho\subsetneq\kappa}V(\theta_\rho)N_\rho(j)$.

Fix $\kappa$ and let $\Delta_\kappa(x)=s_{x+1}(\kappa)-s_x(\kappa)-\sum_{\rho\subsetneq\kappa}V(\theta_\rho)s_x(\rho)$. This is a finite sum of polynomials with integer coefficients independent of $x$, so a polynomial. For integer $j\ge1$, (2) applies to every term, and (3) gives $\Delta_\kappa(j)=0$. Infinitely many zeros, so $\Delta_\kappa\equiv0$. \qed

\emph{Caveats checked.} $s_x$ is defined by the same formula at half-integers, so there is no separate half-integer definition. Thm 1.1(b) and \texttt{lem:peel} are both stated for all $j\ge1$.
\emph{Non-circularity.} The draft's second proof of Thm 1.1 uses $(\star)$+\texttt{lem:peel}; it must keep the K-theoretic proof of $(\star)$. The new proof uses Thm 1.1(b) from \S3. Two proofs of $(\star)$, run in opposite directions, neither circular.

\section*{Checks}
$(\star)$ checked as a polynomial identity in $x$ against type-C Demazure Hilbert polynomials, $V$ brute-forced from the definition (no BR/Thm 4.8 code), $m\le4$ (4, 8, 16 shapes): 0 failures (\texttt{scripts/prove258/star\_poly.py}).
Negative control: adding 1 to any single $V(\theta_\rho)$ breaks $(\star)$ at some integer $j\le m$; all $143/143$ perturbations caught ($m=2$: 6/6, $m=3$: 27/27, $m=4$: 110/110).
