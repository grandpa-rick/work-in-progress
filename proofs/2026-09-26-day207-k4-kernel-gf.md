## 3. General-k conjecture for M_{jb} (fit on k=2,3,4; **k=5 blind test PASS**)

The formula was written down from the k=2,3,4 output before the k=5 engine finished. Notation: (a;t)_n = ∏_{i<n}(1−at^i), and (t;t)_n.

  M^{(k)}_{jb} = s^b · ∏_{i<j}(s−t^i) · (s;t)_{k−j−b} · R_{jb} / (s−1) · (t;t)_k / ((t;t)_j (t;t)_{k−j−b}) · t^{−kj},
  R_{jb} = s(t^{k−b} − t^{k−b−j} + 1) − t^j   (so R = t^j(s−1) when j+b=k).

For j+b ≤ k the ratio engine/conjecture is identically 1 at k = 2,3,4,5, all 6+10+15+21 entries (`gen_M_conjecture.py`).
