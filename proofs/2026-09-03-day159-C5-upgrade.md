## 2. Day 156's lemma is unconditional (Theorem 1)

> **Lemma 2.1.** $M^{(-1)} = \partial X^{(0)} + \tfrac{1}{2}\partial^2\Xi$, where $M := \log H$.

*Proof.* $\log H = (\tau - 1)\log F_P$. Taylor: $\tau = \sum_{k \ge 0} \partial^k/k!$, a finite sum on each
$[T^n]$ since $[T^n]\log F_P$ is a polynomial. Hence $M = \sum_{k \ge 1} \partial^k(\log F_P)/k!$.

Decompose $\log F_P = \sum_{w} (\log F_P)^{(w)}$ into weight-homogeneous layers. By Fact I,
$(\log F_P)^{(w)} = 0$ for $w \ge 2$. So $\log F_P = X^{(0)} + \Xi + \sum_{w \le -1}(\log F_P)^{(w)}$.

$\partial^k$ shifts weight by $-k$. Wt-$(-1)$ contributions to $M$ come only from pairs $(k, w)$ with $w - k = -1$
and $k \ge 1$, i.e. $w = k - 1$:

* $k = 1, w = 0$: $\partial X^{(0)}$
* $k = 2, w = 1$: $(\partial^2/2)\Xi$
* $k \ge 3, w \ge 2$: (log F_P)^{(w)} = 0$

Contributions from wt-$(w \le -1)$ layers of $\log F_P$: for $w \le -1$, the wt-$(-1)$ part of $\partial^k(\log F_P)^{(w)} = \partial^k$ of a wt-$w$ thing lands at wt $w - k \le -1 - 1 = -2$, not wt $-1$; so no contribution.
Combined: $M^{(-1)} = \partial X^{(0)} + \tfrac{1}{2}\partial^2\Xi$. $\square$

**Additionally**, $\partial^2\Xi = \partial(\partial \Xi) = \partial \log \mathcal W$ by Fact II. So
$$M^{(-1)} \;=\; \partial X^{(0)} + \tfrac{1}{2}\partial \log \mathcal W. \tag{2.1}$$

## 6. Why closed-form $\bar D|_{E_3=0}$ needs 3-variable machinery

The obstruction is structural. Two natural closed-form routes both require *3-variable* work:

* **Route A (Riccati at sub-top weight).** Day 158 §3 derived the top-weight and sub-top-weight
  diagonals of $G = F'/F$ at $u_3 = 0$ from the 2nd-order ODE for $F = F_P|_{u_3=0}$. That
  ODE is *specific to $u_3 = 0$*: it comes from $(k+1)c_{k+1} = (u_1+k+1)(u_2+k+1) c_k$, a
  2-variable rising factorial recursion. At $u_3 \ne 0$, $F_P$ satisfies a *Horn/Riccati system*
  (Day 148 Fact I) with $\lambda_i = t_i[\ldots]$ over three $t$-variables. Extracting
  $\partial_{E_3} X^{(0)}|_{E_3=0}$ requires applying $\ell^{\rm top}_0$ to (R) — the *3-variable* Riccati
  — and reading off a coupled linear system for the sub-top diagonals of $\lambda_i$
  (i.e. of $\theta_i S$ for $S = \log \mathcal M$). Day 152 §4 did this at wt-1 to get (P2). Day 159
  does not attempt the wt-0 version.

* **Route B ($\varrho$-decomposition, Day 152 §2).** $\log F_P = \varrho(S) + \varrho\log(\mathcal R/V(u))$
  with $\operatorname{wt}(S) \le 1$ and $\operatorname{wt}(\mathcal R/V(u)) \le 0$. So
  $X^{(0)} = \varrho(S^{(0)}) + \log \varrho(\ell^{\rm top}_0(\mathcal R/V(u)))$. Extracting
  $\ell^{\rm top}_0(\mathcal R/V(u))$ needs one further level of ℓ-top on $V(M)$ and $S$ — again
  3-variable.

Under either route, once $X^{(0)}$ is available in 3-variable closed form, its
$O(E_3)$ Taylor coefficient at $E_3 = 0$ gives $\bar D|_{E_3=0}$, and ($\clubsuit$) closes.

**Day 158's contribution — clarified.** Day 158 provided closed forms for $X^{(0)}|_{u_3=0}$
and $\Xi|_{u_3=0}$. These are *values on the boundary plane* $u_3 = 0$. They do not
determine the *directional derivative* $\partial_{u_3} X^{(0)}|_{u_3=0}$ (equivalently
$\partial_{E_3} X^{(0)}|_{E_3=0}$) which the operator $\partial = \sum \partial_{u_i}$ needs.
