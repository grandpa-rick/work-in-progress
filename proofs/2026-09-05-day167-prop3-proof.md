## Setup

Let $F_P \in \mathbb{Q}[E_1, E_2, E_3][[T]]$ be the standard 3-variable
generating function (Day 152 §1). Assign u-weight $\deg u_i = 1$ so
$\operatorname{wt}(E_1^a E_2^b E_3^c T^n) = a + 2b + 3c - n$. Day 149
Fact II(c) says $\operatorname{wt}(\log F_P) \le 1$, i.e., for each $n \ge 0$,
$[T^n]\log F_P \in \mathbb{Q}[u_1, u_2, u_3]$ has total u-degree $\le n+1$.

Decompose $\log F_P = \sum_{w \le 1} L^{\rm top}_w$ where each $L^{\rm top}_w$
is homogeneous of pure u-weight $w$ (so $[T^n] L^{\rm top}_w$ is homogeneous
in $(u_1, u_2, u_3)$ of total u-degree exactly $n + w$).

* $\Xi := L^{\rm top}_1$ — u-degree $n+1$ at $[T^n]$.
* $X^{(0)} := L^{\rm top}_0$ — u-degree $n$ at $[T^n]$.
* $X^{(-1)} := L^{\rm top}_{-1}$ — u-degree $n-1$ at $[T^n]$.
* $X^{(-w)} := L^{\rm top}_{-w}$ — u-degree $n-w$ at $[T^n]$, for $w \ge 0$.

Rick's convention: $R^{(-1)}_n := \partial_{u_3} X^{(0)}_n \big|_{u_3=0}$
(a polynomial in $(u_1, u_2)$ of degree $n-1$). Prop 3's LHS.

Restriction notation: $F_c := F_P|_{u_3 = c}$ (a 2-variable series in $T$
with coefficients in $\mathbb{Q}[u_1, u_2]$).

## Statement

**Prop 3.** For all $n \ge 2$,
$$R^{(-1)}_n \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\, \partial_{u_3}^2 \Xi_n\big|_{u_3 = 0}
\;-\; \Bigl[\deg_{(u_1, u_2)} = n - 1\Bigr]\Bigl([T^n]\log(F_{-1}/F_0)\Bigr).$$

## Proof

### Step 1 — Layer contribution to the degree-$(n-1)$ part of $\log F_c$

Fix $c \in \mathbb{Q}$. For any layer $L^{\rm top}_w$, decompose
$[T^n]L^{\rm top}_w$ by powers of $u_3$:
$$[T^n]L^{\rm top}_w \;=\; \sum_{k \ge 0} u_3^k \cdot Q_{w, n, k}(u_1, u_2),$$
where each $Q_{w, n, k}$ is homogeneous in $(u_1, u_2)$ of degree
$(n+w) - k$. (Follows from total-u-degree $= n + w$ homogeneity.)

Substituting $u_3 = c$:
$$[T^n]L^{\rm top}_w\Big|_{u_3 = c} \;=\; \sum_{k \ge 0} c^k \, Q_{w, n, k}(u_1, u_2),$$
a polynomial in $(u_1, u_2)$ that is a sum of homogeneous pieces of degrees
$\{n + w - k : k \ge 0, \; k \le n + w\}$.

Taking the degree-$(n-1)$ piece requires $n + w - k = n - 1$, i.e., $k = w + 1$.
This requires $k \ge 0$, i.e., $w \ge -1$.

Combined with $w \le 1$ from Fact II(c), only three layers contribute:

| $w$ | $k = w + 1$ | Contribution to $[\deg = n-1] [T^n] L^{\rm top}_w|_{u_3 = c}$ |
|---|---|---|
| $1$ | $2$ | $c^2 \cdot Q_{1, n, 2} = c^2 \cdot (u_3^2\text{-coeff of }\Xi_n)$ |
| $0$ | $1$ | $c \cdot Q_{0, n, 1} = c \cdot (u_3\text{-coeff of }X^{(0)}_n)$ |
| $-1$ | $0$ | $Q_{-1, n, 0} = X^{(-1)}_n|_{u_3 = 0}$ (the pure-$(u_1, u_2)$ part of $X^{(-1)}_n$) |
| $\le -2$ | $\le -1$ | $0$ (no valid $k \ge 0$) |

Summing over $w$:
$$[\deg = n-1]\bigl([T^n]\log F_P\bigr)\big|_{u_3 = c} \;=\;
\tfrac{c^2}{2!}\, \partial_{u_3}^2 \Xi_n\big|_0
\;+\; c \, \partial_{u_3} X^{(0)}_n\big|_0
\;+\; X^{(-1)}_n\big|_{u_3 = 0}, \tag{$\star$}$$
using $\partial_{u_3}^k L^{\rm top}_w\big|_{u_3=0} = k! \cdot Q_{w, n, k}$.
The first two terms are pure $(u_1, u_2)$-polynomials of degree $n - 1$; the
third is $X^{(-1)}_n|_{u_3=0}$, which is homogeneous of $(u_1, u_2)$-degree
$n - 1$ (since $X^{(-1)}_n$ is homogeneous of u-degree $n-1$, and setting
$u_3 = 0$ drops nothing but forbids nonzero $u_3$-powers).

### Step 2 — Evaluate ($\star$) at $c = 0$ and $c = -1$

At $c = 0$: $c^2 = 0$, $c = 0$, so
$$[\deg = n-1]\bigl([T^n]\log F_0\bigr) \;=\; X^{(-1)}_n\big|_{u_3 = 0}. \tag{L$_0$}$$

At $c = -1$: $c^2 = 1$, $c = -1$, so
$$[\deg = n-1]\bigl([T^n]\log F_{-1}\bigr) \;=\;
\tfrac{1}{2}\, \partial_{u_3}^2 \Xi_n\big|_0
\;-\; \partial_{u_3} X^{(0)}_n\big|_0
\;+\; X^{(-1)}_n\big|_{u_3 = 0}. \tag{L$_{-1}$}$$

Recognising $\partial_{u_3} X^{(0)}_n|_0 = R^{(-1)}_n$ by definition.

### Step 3 — Subtract

Subtracting (L$_0$) from (L$_{-1}$), the $X^{(-1)}_n|_{u_3 = 0}$ term cancels:
$$[\deg = n-1]\bigl([T^n]\log(F_{-1}/F_0)\bigr) \;=\;
\tfrac{1}{2}\, \partial_{u_3}^2 \Xi_n\big|_0
\;-\; R^{(-1)}_n.$$

Note $\log(F_{-1}/F_0) = \log F_{-1} - \log F_0$ as formal power series (both
$F_0$ and $F_{-1}$ have constant term $1$: $F_0[T^0] = F_P[T^0]|_{u_3=0} = 1$
and $F_{-1}[T^0] = F_P[T^0]|_{u_3=-1} = 1$).

Solving for $R^{(-1)}_n$:
$$\boxed{\;R^{(-1)}_n \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\, \partial_{u_3}^2 \Xi_n\big|_{u_3 = 0}
\;-\; \bigl[\deg_{(u_1, u_2)} = n - 1\bigr]\bigl([T^n]\log(F_{-1}/F_0)\bigr).\;}$$

This is Prop 3. $\square$

## Method notes

The proof is a pure weight-grading argument. It uses ONLY:

1. Day 149 Fact II(c): $\operatorname{wt}(\log F_P) \le 1$ (proved).
2. Total u-degree homogeneity of each weight-graded layer $L^{\rm top}_w$
   (definition of the weight grading).
3. Definitions: $\Xi := L^{\rm top}_1$, $X^{(0)} := L^{\rm top}_0$,
   $X^{(-1)} := L^{\rm top}_{-1}$, $R^{(-1)} := \partial_{u_3} X^{(0)}|_{u_3=0}$.
4. Elementary polynomial algebra (substitution $u_3 \mapsto c$).

No Riccati algebra, no $\nu$-system, no external theorems.

The KEY insight is that at target $(u_1, u_2)$-degree $m = n - 1$, EXACTLY
three layers ($w = 1, 0, -1$) contribute, and their contributions at $c = 0$
and $c = -1$ differ by only two terms (the $c$-dependent ones), so subtracting
kills the $X^{(-1)}$ term entirely.

This is a clean Rule-11 firing: unfold the definition of "degree-$(n-1)$
part of $\log F_c$" through the weight decomposition, and both slices
factor through the same $X^{(-1)}$-contribution which cancels.
