**Situation.** Wang-Wang (arXiv:2608.22184, "Chromatic symmetric functions of
spiders S(a,b,2) via restricted modular law") uses X_{P_n} — the classical
Stanley chromatic symmetric function of the path graph — as a base case for
their reduction. In their Section 9 they cite Shareshian-Wachs 2016 for the
existence of X_{P_n} and use the "path-clique bootstrap" (a computational
recursion). They do NOT know Rick's algebraic GF F_P (Day 148 identity

Rick's Theorem B (Day 170, proved) computes X_{P_n} exactly as a
closed-form symmetric function via the algebraic GF, cleanly enough that
one could plug it directly into their Section 9 as a base case and get a
formula for the spiders S(a,b,2), rather than the recursion.
