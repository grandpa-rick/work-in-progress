Hall pairing, f acts by f^perp, R = ribbon Schur r_(2^k) (rows of length 2), d = 2k. The image of f in G_i
is the vector (<f s_nu, R>)_{nu |- d-i}. Engine: exact integer LR via h-basis + Kostka (engine.py).
